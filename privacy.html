Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Summary

Business Reporting is an accounting app for iPhone, developed by Adil Habeeb. It does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Your data stays on your iPhone

Companies, accounts, cost centres, journals, customers, vendors, employees and entries are stored only on your device, protected by iOS file encryption. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and it makes no network connections. The developer never receives your data, your usage or any identifier for your device.

Backups and reports you share

Backups and PDF or Excel reports are created on your iPhone. They leave it only when you choose to share them, for example to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or Mail. The service you choose then holds that copy under its own privacy terms.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one company and its books, open Masters › Company and choose Delete.
  • To delete everything, delete the app. This removes all of its data and the backups kept on your iPhone.
  • Copies you saved elsewhere, such as in iCloud Drive, stay until you delete them there.

Children

Business Reporting is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and shown in the app under Masters › Privacy & about, with a new effective date.
