Getting started

On first launch, enter your company name, currency, fiscal year and VAT rate. You get a ready chart of accounts, journals and cost centres, all editable in Masters. To look around first, tap Explore with sample data.

Back up your books

  • The app makes a backup on your iPhone once a day when something has changed.
  • Open Masters › Backup & restore and tap Back up now to save a copy to iCloud Drive or Files, or to AirDrop or email it.
  • Backups kept on your iPhone are deleted with the app, so keep a copy in iCloud Drive.

Move to a new iPhone or restore

Install the app on the new iPhone, tap Restore from a backup on the welcome screen, and choose your .ledgerbackup file. You can also tap the backup file in Files, Mail or AirDrop. You can replace all data, or add the backup's companies next to the ones you have.

Reports

Reports gives you the Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Financial Performance, Statement of Cash Flows and Trial Balance for any period, with comparative columns. Export them as a PDF or an Excel workbook from the share button.

Locking a closed period

Open Masters › Company and turn on the period lock. Entries on or before the lock date can't be posted, edited or deleted until you move the date back.

Your privacy

Your books stay on your iPhone. The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
